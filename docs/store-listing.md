# Store listing copy — Ceria

Draft copy for Google Play and the Apple App Store, in English and Indonesian.
Everything here is a draft: **the Indonesian still needs the reviewer's pass**,
the same as the in-app text. Character counts are given because both stores
truncate silently.

Two rules this copy follows, from Ceria's own values:

- **Nothing is claimed that the app does not do.** No "transform your family in
  30 days", no invented statistics, no implied endorsement.
- **The paid part is stated plainly, up front.** A parent should know what is
  free and what costs money before they install, not after they are attached to
  it. Both stores require the disclosure; we would want it anyway.

### Full description — 4,000 characters

**Indonesian**

```
Ceria menemani Anda menjadi orang tua yang Anda inginkan — pelan-pelan, satu
hari sekali.

Dibuat oleh Yayasan Sukacita Keluarga Indonesia, sebuah yayasan nirlaba di
Surabaya yang mendampingi keluarga sejak bertahun-tahun. Isinya bukan teori
dari negeri lain, melainkan yang kami pelajari bersama orang tua Indonesia.

DI DALAMNYA ADA TIGA HAL

Panduan — 12 bab tentang mengasuh anak: batasan yang jelas, marah tanpa
melukai, mendengarkan, memperbaiki hubungan setelah bertengkar. Setiap bab
dipecah menjadi bacaan harian singkat, 365 hari, cukup beberapa menit sehari.

Alat — 12 lembar kerja yang bisa Anda isi: kesepakatan keluarga, daftar
pemicu marah, rencana waktu berdua dengan anak, dan lainnya. Bisa diisi
berdua dengan pasangan.

Jurnal — 52 minggu untuk menulis. Ada ruang terpisah untuk Mama dan Papa,
supaya masing-masing bisa jujur pada dirinya sendiri.

YANG GRATIS DAN YANG BERBAYAR

Bab 1 sampai 4 gratis selamanya: empat bab panduan, 121 bacaan harian,
empat lembar kerja, dan jurnal minggu 1 sampai 16. Cukup untuk empat bulan
pertama.

Untuk membuka bab 5 sampai 12 ada satu kali pembayaran — sekali bayar,
terbuka selamanya, bukan langganan. Tidak ada iklan.

Kalau Anda ganti HP atau memasang ulang, tekan "Pulihkan pembelian" dan bab
Anda kembali. Tidak perlu bayar dua kali.

TULISAN ANDA MILIK ANDA

Semua yang Anda tulis tersimpan di HP Anda sendiri. Kami tidak bisa
membacanya. Tidak ada akun, tidak ada pendaftaran, tidak perlu nomor
telepon.

Kalau Anda mau punya cadangan, Anda bisa menghubungkan Google Drive Anda
sendiri — dan Anda bisa mematikannya kapan saja. Itu Drive Anda, bukan
server kami.

BAHASA

Bahasa Indonesia dan Inggris. Bisa diganti kapan saja, dan tulisan Anda
tetap utuh.

TENTANG NADANYA

Tidak ada hitungan mundur, tidak ada tanda seru merah, tidak ada yang
menyalahkan Anda kalau satu hari terlewat. Hari yang terlewat memang
terlewat. Besok Anda bisa mulai lagi.

Yayasan Sukacita Keluarga Indonesia (CERIA)
Surabaya, Indonesia
```

**English**

```
Ceria walks with you towards the parent you want to be — slowly, one day at a
time.

It is made by Yayasan Sukacita Keluarga Indonesia, a non-profit foundation in
Surabaya that has sat with families for years. What is inside is not theory
borrowed from somewhere else; it is what we have learned alongside Indonesian
parents.

THREE THINGS INSIDE

The guide — 12 chapters on raising children: clear limits, being angry
without wounding, listening, and repairing things after a fight. Each chapter
is broken into short daily readings, 365 of them, a few minutes a day.

The toolkit — 12 worksheets you fill in yourself: a family agreement, a list
of what sets your temper off, a plan for time alone with each child, and
more. You and your partner can fill them in together.

The diary — 52 weeks to write in, with separate space for Mama and Papa, so
each of you can be honest with yourself.

WHAT IS FREE AND WHAT IS PAID

Chapters 1 to 4 are free for good: four chapters of the guide, 121 daily
readings, four worksheets, and diary weeks 1 to 16. That is the first four
months.

Chapters 5 to 12 unlock with one payment — paid once, yours for good, not a
subscription. There are no ads.

If you change phones or reinstall, tap "Restore purchases" and your chapters
come back. You never pay twice.

WHAT YOU WRITE IS YOURS

Everything you write stays on your own phone. We cannot read it. There is no
account, no sign-up, and no phone number to give.

If you want a backup, you can connect your own Google Drive — and turn it off
again whenever you like. It is your Drive, not our server.

LANGUAGES

Indonesian and English. Switch whenever you like; your writing stays exactly
as it was.

ABOUT THE TONE

There are no countdowns, no red exclamation marks, and nothing that scolds
you for missing a day. A missed day is just a missed day. Tomorrow you can
begin again.

Yayasan Sukacita Keluarga Indonesia (CERIA)
Surabaya, Indonesia
```
